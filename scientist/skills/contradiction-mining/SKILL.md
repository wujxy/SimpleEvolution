# contradiction_mining — two rights make a new fact

Load this when two results you have reason to trust refuse to
coexist. The ordinary reflexes — pick one, average them, declare
"both may be right" and move on — each destroy the most informative
object in the room. Two verified facts in contradiction are a
boundary marker of a hidden structure.

## The mining

With result A saying X and result B saying not-X, ask in order:

1. Conditions — where exactly do their regimes differ? A
   contradiction inside one regime is an error; across two regimes is
   a discovery.
2. Hidden variable — what changed between the two measurements that
   nobody controlled? The contradiction is that variable announcing
   itself.
3. Regime transition — is there a phase boundary between them? Then
   locating it is the result.
4. Latent quantities — are A and B actually measuring different
   underlying quantities that only look like the same one?
5. The covering model — what single mechanism makes both A and B
   true as stated? That mechanism is the candidate discovery.

## The pairing

Contradiction-mining creates the explanation;
cheapest-discriminating-experiment decides it. The mined model
predicts where the boundary sits and which knob moves it — write
that fork and run it before believing either the contradiction or
the resolution.
