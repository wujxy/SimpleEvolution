# hypothesis_generation_and_evolution — making directions that are actually new

Load this when fresh ideas have stopped coming but candidates and
partial results are on the table — and whenever a new direction is
needed, at generation time rather than after. Fresh sampling from the
same context yields variations; new directions come from operators
applied to what you already hold.

## The operators

On existing candidates, apply:

- recombination — A's mechanism in B's carrier;
- simplification — remove a part; if it still holds, the part was
  decoration and the simpler form is the real mechanism;
- inversion — fix what was free, free what was fixed; swap input for
  output; negate the objective;
- abstraction shift — are A and B instances of one deeper mechanism?
  work the mechanism instead of the instances;
- constraint relaxation — which constraint, read one notch looser
  (see wall_foundation_attack), opens what space?
- new mechanism import — bring in a mechanism from elsewhere, by the
  structured route: write the mapping first (this X ≡ that Y), then
  port the mechanism — not the vocabulary.

Keep lineage: each variant records its parent and the one part
changed. A lineage is debuggable — a failure localizes to the part —
and a pile of unrelated candidates is not.

## The filter

Before any candidate earns work, four questions: is it testable
(what observation kills it)? is it novel (against the dead lanes and
banked candidates — a revived dead lane needs a stated reason it
stays dead no longer)? is it feasible at acceptable cost? what is
its expected information gain if true — and if false?

Generation has not stalled while the operators still pay; only when
they stop is it time for research_expansion's wider force.
