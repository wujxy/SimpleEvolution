# delegation — the craft of working through colleagues

Load this when framing work for a colleague, when one seems adrift or
stuck, or when the collaboration itself feels wrong.

## Framing: a goal, not a task

State the work as a goal — what constitutes done, the hard
constraints, the stop conditions (when to report stuck rather than
grind), the evidence a claim must carry — and leave the how to the
colleague. An underspecified brief cannot be watched: drift is only
visible against a stated goal. An overspecified one spends your turns
deciding what their judgment was for.

For work you know this class of colleague handles well —
implementation, measurement, reproduction, the long grind inside a
frame — open the engagement first and study the terrain in parallel:
your understanding catches up with the running colleague rather than
gating them. What you keep regardless: what matters, what is enough,
the record, the gates, and when to stop.

## Watching, nudging, re-chartering

With a mainline working a goal brief — a long box, carried forward
through continue_engagement, for accumulated context is where depth
comes from — your involvement is priced in three steep tiers.

Watching is free: wait in bounded slices, and between them read what
the colleague has laid down — the transcript the acknowledgment
points to, the diff in the tree, the gates they ran.

A nudge is an interruption earned by evidence: drift (a failed gate,
work departing the stated goal) or a stall (their own report, or
gates without a new best) — never unease. The channel:
cancel_engagement, then continue_engagement with the correction as
the brief; the session and the tree survive it, one in-flight step
does not.

When nudges stop landing, re-charter at the leg boundary. Diagnose
before redirecting: a Challenger attacking the stuck claim, a
Proposer on a different mechanism, see what the stuck context cannot
— then open the next leg fresh, carrying the diagnosis. Speculative
seats open between mainline engagements, with the tree standing at a
committed state; a fork taken mid-edit inherits half-finished work.

## The two ledgers

Experiments: a small probe and a full evaluation answer different
questions. The probe — run at smaller scale wherever the evaluation
takes one — ranks candidates; only the full evaluation decides, and a
promotion that skips it is not efficiency. In a bit-exact world a
correctness check on a small sample is exact; speed transfers as
ordering, never as value. Measurement has a noise floor: repeat a
fixed state, learn its spread, and read sub-noise deltas as ties. A
probe is only cheap when it is narrow — a filesystem-wide search over
network mounts costs minutes to hours; scope the path, or hand the
hunt to a bounded engagement.

Colleagues: the expensive asset is a colleague who has read the code
and learned its traps — continue them, and give the deep cuts to them
alone. A fresh colleague costs their brief, not the codebase: hand
them the relevant functions and the measured profile, and let their
first engagement be bounded. What is worth keeping lands in commits
and notes as it happens — a context dies with its engagement, the
artifacts do not.

And the delegation boundary itself moves with evidence. When
colleagues keep choosing better than your briefs assumed, widen
their freedom; when they keep foundering on framing, narrow it or
take the task back.
