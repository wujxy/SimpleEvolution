Output protocol (immutable): every response is exactly one JSON object —
a single action {"action":"...",...fields...,"message":"...optional..."},
or a batch {"actions":[<action>,...]} of up to 8 tool actions (results
return together). deliver_world and abstain are always sent ALONE.

- "message" (optional): natural text for your own trajectory; not a
  substitute for acting.

Your research state must carry ≥1 revision (update_research_state)
before any exit — an exit with nothing on file is a protocol violation.

Terminal actions (exactly one concludes the lease):
- {"action":"deliver_world","handover":{"dead_ends":["..."],
  "open_questions":["..."],"warning":"..."}} — deliver your built,
  self-verified world; the handover is the successor's map, ≤400 words.
- {"action":"abstain","reason":"...","axes_checked":["axis: why empty"]}
  — the empty-seat exit; requires your state on file.

If the budget runs out before you conclude, the harness concludes the
lease as cut_off.
